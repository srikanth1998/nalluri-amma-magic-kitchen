# Nalluri Foods: Amma's Magic

Create a visually rich, modern yet traditional South Indian food brand website for a company named “Nalluri Foods”.

Brand Identity:

 Theme: Authentic, homely, traditional South Indian

 Tagline: “Amma’s Pure Magic”

 Feel: Warm, nostalgic, cultural, homemade

 Colors: Deep royal blue, turmeric yellow, vermillion red, earthy tones

 Style inspiration: Traditional Indian art, temple patterns, festive elements

Homepage:

 Hero section with a traditional illustration of a South Indian woman cooking (like the provided image style)

 Headline: Authentic Flavors, Feels Like Home 🏠

 Subtext: Handmade with love, rooted in tradition

 CTA buttons: “Shop Now” / “Explore Products”

Sections to Include:

1. About Us

 Story about homemade recipes passed down generations

 Emphasize authenticity, purity, and traditional cooking methods

2. Products Section (Grid Layout)

Categories:

 Sambar Karam

 Goddu Karam

 Podulu (Spice Powders)

 Veg Pickles

 Non-Veg Pickles

 Sweets

 Snacks

Each product card should have:

 Image

 Short description

 “Buy Now” button

3. Why Choose Us

 Homemade & authentic

 No preservatives

 Traditional recipes

 Freshly prepared

 Worldwide shipping

4. Shipping Section

 Highlight: “Shipping across India & International ✈️”

 Mention safe packaging and fast delivery

5. Testimonials

 Customer reviews about taste and homely feeling

6. Contact / Order Section

 WhatsApp integration

 Instagram link

 Simple order form

Design Requirements:

 Use traditional Indian patterns, borders, and textures

 Include food photography with rich colors

 Use elegant serif + clean sans-serif font combination

 Add subtle animations (hover effects, fade-ins)

 Mobile-friendly responsive design

Footer:

 Logo + tagline

 Quick links

 Social media icons

 Contact info
